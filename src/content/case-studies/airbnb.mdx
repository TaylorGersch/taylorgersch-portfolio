<TwoCol
  overview="Project Atrium was a 14-month engagement to modernize Airbnb's internal customer support platform, replacing pieces of the legacy Nova Tickets system and building out a new design language made specifically for internal tooling. I worked as part of a three-person design team embedded with Airbnb's Studio Lima organization, partnering closely with Beth Soucy (XD Lead) and Cassidy Dutcher (Product Manager) across four major releases — Copper, Baymax, Global, and a final Design QA pass. Organizing Cases was one of the flagship pieces of that work: a full redesign of the case inbox every support ambassador used to manage their queue."
  problem="Ambassadors couldn't easily tell what actually needed their attention. Cases lived in a single flat list with no reliable way to distinguish a brand-new case from one still waiting on a guest or host reply, so ambassadors were opening cases one by one just to figure out where to focus — a slow way to work through a queue that could run dozens of cases deep."
/>

<Pair>

<Detail title="The process">

I mapped out every state a case could move through — brand new and unopened, a guest or host reply that hadn't been read yet, one that had been read, one an ambassador had already responded to — and defined exactly how each should look and behave: what stayed bold, what showed a status dot, how a card looked on hover versus once selected. Getting this system right mattered more than any single screen, since ambassadors were scanning this list dozens of times a day.

</Detail>

<Detail title="The solution">

A rebuilt case inbox split into Open and Pending tabs, so ambassadors could separate cases needing action from ones waiting on someone else. A consistent visual hierarchy across every case state, so a glance at the list shows exactly where attention is needed first. And a hover state that previews a case's reservation and message thread without opening it, letting ambassadors triage faster before committing to a full case view.

</Detail>

</Pair>

<div className="border-t border-neutral-200 pt-8">

<Detail title="The outcome">

The redesigned inbox shipped with the Copper release and became the default way every Airbnb ambassador worked their queue. Across the full Atrium engagement, the team went on to ship more than 30 individual features, stand up a shared notification framework, and leave behind reusable hand-off and QA processes that Airbnb's internal design org adopted going forward.

</Detail>

</div>
