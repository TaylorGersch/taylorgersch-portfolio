<TwoCol
  overview="Coach tooling is an internal, coach-facing product within BetterUp — not something members ever see. I was the sole designer on this team, and unlike the D2C work, there wasn't a dedicated product manager. I identified the opportunity myself, then drove both the MVP and the roadmap for future improvements. The initial version shipped in about three weeks."
  problem="Coaches were walking into sessions without enough context on the member they were meeting. They had no quick way to see a member's focus areas, coaching style, or even simple details like time zone and location — small things that matter for the first few minutes of small talk before a session gets going. On top of that, coaches were spending significant time after each session writing up notes just to keep everything straight for the next one. This came mostly from coach-reported feedback, backed by some supporting data."
