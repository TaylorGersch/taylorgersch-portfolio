<TwoCol
  overview="As an embedded product designer on BetterUp's D2C team, I led a two-week sprint to redesign how prospective members find and choose a coach. I was the sole designer on the project, working closely with a product manager who surfaced the drop-off data behind it, and partnering directly with engineering to rework the matching logic."
  problem="Members were rematching with coaches far more often than expected, and drop-off data showed people abandoning the flow before ever booking a session. The existing coach selection screen only offered a handful of basic filters, and the matching logic behind it wasn't sophisticated enough to recommend coaches that actually fit what someone needed."
/>

<Pair>

<Detail title="The process">

I researched how other matching-based products — therapy platforms, academic mentor-pairing tools — handle the same problem: helping someone choose the right person for a high-stakes, personal relationship. That research shaped how I thought about filter categories and what information a member actually needs to trust a match.

</Detail>

<Detail title="The solution">

New filter categories, built around how members actually think about their goals (not just credentials or availability)

Reworked backend matching logic, developed in close collaboration with engineering, to surface better-fit coaches

A redesigned coach profile, giving members more context to make a confident choice

</Detail>

</Pair>

<div className="border-t border-neutral-200 pt-8">

<Detail title="The outcome">

Coach rematching rates dropped by 45%. The new matching logic was strong enough that other teams across BetterUp adopted it for their own products — a good signal that the approach worked beyond just this one flow.

</Detail>

</div>

<MediaSplit eyebrow="Coach Tooling" title="Coach Session Prep & In-Call Context" titleGapClassName="mb-[72px]" image="/images/betterup-coach-session.webp" imageAlt="Coach session prep & in-call context — desktop + mobile mockups" imageWidth="1500" imageHeight="1595" side="left">

<TwoCol
  overview="Coach tooling is an internal, coach-facing product within BetterUp — not something members ever see. I was the sole designer on this team, and unlike the D2C work, there wasn't a dedicated product manager. I identified the opportunity myself, then drove both the MVP and the roadmap for future improvements. The initial version shipped in about three weeks."
  problem="Coaches were walking into sessions without enough context on the member they were meeting. They had no quick way to see a member's focus areas, coaching style, or even simple details like time zone and location — small things that matter for the first few minutes of small talk before a session gets going. On top of that, coaches were spending significant time after each session writing up notes just to keep everything straight for the next one. This came mostly from coach-reported feedback, backed by some supporting data."
/>

<Detail title="The solution">

An at-a-glance member overview, surfaced before and during the session — focus areas, preferred coaching style, company, program details, and useful context like time zone and location

An opt-in "Session Insights" feature, clearly labeled around privacy: notes stay confidential to the coach, and session recordings stay confidential to the member if they've opted in

An in-call sidebar with Overview and Notes tabs, letting coaches add notes and reference relevant materials in real time — without leaving the call or losing presence with the member

I'm especially proud of the in-call integration — it meant coaches could stay focused on the conversation instead of splitting attention between the call and a separate notes doc.

</Detail>

<div className="border-t border-neutral-200 pt-8">

<Detail title="The outcome">

There's no hard metric to point to here, but the first rollout got strong positive feedback from coaches, and the internal team saw real buy-in — people understood the value of surfacing this context immediately.

</Detail>

</div>
