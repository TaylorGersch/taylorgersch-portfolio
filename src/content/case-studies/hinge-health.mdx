<Detail title="The outcome">

Across the three research tracks, we collected data on what Hinge Health's competitors were doing well versus where the opportunities were for a stand-alone product, spanning product and design, growth and marketing, and content and messaging.

Halfway through the engagement, we presented these studies back to the relevant Hinge Health teams to align the group on Medicare members' motivators and blockers ahead of the north star design work.

<TwoCol
  overview="With the research phase complete, I moved into north star vision design focused specifically on Medicare Advantage. Hinge Health already offered physical therapy advice, routines, and education to other audience segments — this phase was about identifying the onboarding opportunities for members 65 and older specifically."
  problem="Members 65+ don't share the same needs, technology comfort, or entry points as Hinge Health's existing audience, so the team needed a north star vision for onboarding to a new product that's built around this population specifically, rather than adapting what already existed."
/>

<Pair>

<Detail title="The solution">

We identified the main engagement goal for this product was to support Medicare members' goals of safely aging in their homes and communities by providing a product that's easy to integrate into their day-to-day routines.

</Detail>

<Detail title="The outcome">

I was able to identify 3 significant areas of focus that honed in on multiple facets of health: physical, mental, intellectual, and social. We developed product features that encourage physical health such as guided stretching videos and community gathering and empowerment within the product via personalized accessibility features for the Medicare Advantage group.

</Detail>

</Pair>
