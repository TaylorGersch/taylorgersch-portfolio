Every contract can flex on currency, geography, timespan, and other negotiated terms, so the tool needed to be flexible enough to handle that complexity while still streamlining the day-to-day workflow for the people using it. I partnered with Stripe's product manager to define the product work, then collaborated directly with the tool's primary users to shape a workflow that actually worked better for them."
  problem="There wasn't yet a tool built around how this team actually worked, and many of the contracts Stripe signs with businesses require their own customizations, which made a flexible, purpose-built solution even more essential."

<Pair>

<Detail title="The process">

I spent my time interviewing the primary users of the internal tool, asking specific workflow questions to better understand what kind of functionality they'd need moving forward. Alongside those conversations, I got familiar with Stripe's existing design system and identified where I could adapt existing components to feel more purpose-built for this kind of internal, day-to-day tool.

</Detail>

<Detail title="The solution">

The result is a highly customizable contract tool that gives Stripe's go-to-market team the flexibility to shape each deal around what actually works, for Stripe and for the business on the other side of the table. Rather than forcing every negotiation into the same rigid template, the tool adapts to each contract's specific terms, so the team can show up for existing and prospective customers in a way that fits both parties.

</Detail>

</Pair>

<TwoCol
  overview="Stripe Connections lets consumers link their bank accounts to Stripe, giving them — or the merchants they're interacting with — access to a range of product experiences, from processing ACH payments to extending credit to personal finance management. I worked with Stripe's product manager to define the user stories and corresponding prototypes, then built out a large demo to walk the executive team through the experience."
  problem="Before Stripe Connections, authentication flows were inconsistent across direct connections, OAuth connections, underwriting services, and micro-deposits. This work established a consistent look, feel, timing, and flow across all Stripe-related authentication workflows."
/>

<Pair>

<Detail title="The process">

Before designing the flows themselves, I did extensive competitive analysis to understand best practices across the industry. From that research, I identified the need for a consistent Stripe look and feel that still gave individual merchants enough flexibility to white-label the experience if they chose to.

</Detail>

<Detail title="The solution">

I designed one cohesive flow that carries a user from consent through bank selection, secure authentication, and account verification — consistent whether the underlying connection is direct, OAuth-based, an underwriting service, or micro-deposits. It's embeddable directly into merchant checkout, and handles both single- and multi-account households so users can pick exactly which account to connect.

</Detail>

</Pair>
