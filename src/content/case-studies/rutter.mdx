<ContentSplit eyebrow="Product Design" title="Data Sync, made simple">

<SplitColumn>

<Detail title="Overview + Role">

Rutter is a unified API that connects B2B software to the accounting, commerce, ads and banking platforms their customers use, like QuickBooks, Xero and NetSuite. The Data Sync Configuration page controls how and when that data arrives, but new customers couldn't set it up on their own and relied on Customer Success to get started.

I redesigned the page around how customers actually set up, and added the dashboard's first guided walkthrough, working alongside a product manager.

</Detail>

<Detail title="The problem">

New customers couldn't understand the page, and they couldn't make it do what they needed.

- **Hard to understand:** two kinds of sync, layered settings, and sandbox vs. production, with nothing on the page explaining any of it.
- **Hard to control:** every customer needed different data, at a different frequency, for different reasons.

</Detail>

</SplitColumn>
